What Is Website Maintenance and Why Does It Matter?

Website maintenance keeps your site secure, fast, reliable, and working properly through ongoing updates and issue resolution. Without proactive care, aging pages, routine edits, software conflicts, human errors, bots, and hacking attempts can slow your site down, create vulnerabilities, or cause important features to fail. Our plans provide expert support and the right amount of monthly maintenance time based on what your website actually needs.

WordPress Website Maintenance

WordPress websites depend on the core platform, themes, plugins, and third-party integrations working together correctly. Our WordPress maintenance plans keep security-related software updated while identifying and resolving errors, conflicts, performance problems, broken functionality, and responsive display issues.

Sites with many plugins, custom functionality, or significant accumulated issues may require additional Pro Maintenance hours.

Drupal Website Maintenance

Drupal websites often require more specialized maintenance because updates may involve Drupal core, contributed modules, custom modules, themes, permissions, caching, server compatibility, and complex content structures.

Larger or highly customized Drupal websites will typically require more monthly hours because they have more components, dependencies, and potential issues to review and resolve.

When Maintenance Is Not Enough

Some older websites have outdated code, incompatible components, broken templates, or years of accumulated problems. In certain cases, repairing everything would cost more than rebuilding the website correctly. When that happens, we will recommend the most practical path: continued maintenance, a larger cleanup project, or a new website.

Website Maintenance FAQs

What Does Website Maintenance Include?

Security & Updates Only covers essential WordPress or Drupal security updates and critical patches. Standard and Pro plans also include quarterly website health audits and monthly time for issue resolution, performance and stability problems, mobile issues, and other website repairs.

What Is Included in a Quarterly Website Health Audit?

The audit identifies errors, performance concerns, mobile issues, broken functionality, and other problems that may have developed. Findings are prioritized, and resolution work uses the monthly hours included in the plan.

Why Does My Website Have So Many Reported Issues?

Websites accumulate issues as software, pages, integrations, and technology change. Audit tools may also count the same recurring problem on every affected page, quickly producing hundreds or thousands of reported issues.

Do You Fix Every Issue Found in an Audit?

We address the issues that matter most first, based on their effect on security, speed, stability, functionality, conversions, and user experience. Larger issue volumes are resolved over time based on the monthly hours included in the plan.

How Many Monthly Maintenance Hours Can I Choose?

Standard Website Maintenance includes two hours per month. Pro Website Maintenance allows you to choose 3–50+ hours per month—or as much time as your website needs.

Are Hosting, Backups, and Uptime Monitoring Included?

Hosting, server-level backups, and uptime monitoring are part of the website’s hosting service and are not listed as maintenance-plan features.

Is Technical SEO Included?

Technical SEO strategy, indexing, schema, internal linking, content optimization, and AI search optimization are handled through our separate SEO + AIO services.
