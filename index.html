What Is Website Maintenance and Why Does It Matter?

Website maintenance is the ongoing process of keeping your site secure, fast, and working properly. Without it, software conflicts, aging code, and hacking attempts create problems that compound over time.

Our WordPress and Drupal website maintenance plans provide expert support with the right amount of monthly time for your website.

It is common for an established business website to have hundreds of reported issues, while older, larger, or more complex websites may have thousands.

WordPress Website Maintenance

WordPress websites depend on the core platform, themes, plugins, and third-party integrations working together correctly. Our WordPress website maintenance plans keep security-related software updated while identifying and resolving errors, conflicts, performance problems, broken functionality, and responsive display issues.

Sites with many plugins, custom functionality, or significant accumulated issues may require additional Pro Maintenance hours.

Drupal Website Maintenance

Drupal websites often require more specialized maintenance because updates may involve Drupal core, contributed modules, custom modules, themes, permissions, caching, server compatibility, and complex content structures.

Larger or highly customized Drupal websites will typically require more monthly hours because they have more components, dependencies, and potential issues to review and resolve.

Website Maintenance FAQs

What Does Website Maintenance Include?

Security Updates covers essential WordPress or Drupal security patches for core, plugins, modules, and themes. Managed Website Maintenance provides 1–20 dedicated hours of professional web maintenance per month for issue resolution, performance and stability problems, compatibility, functionality, and other website repairs. Technical SEO covers crawling, indexing, schema, site structure, and search performance.

What Is Included in a Quarterly Website Health Audit?

The audit identifies errors, performance concerns, mobile issues, broken functionality, and other problems that may have developed. Findings are prioritized, and resolution work uses the monthly hours included in the plan.

Why Does My Website Have So Many Reported Issues?

Websites accumulate issues as software, pages, integrations, and technology change. Audit tools may also count the same recurring problem on every affected page, quickly producing hundreds or thousands of reported issues.

Do You Fix Every Issue Found in an Audit?

We address the issues that matter most first, based on their effect on security, speed, stability, functionality, conversions, and user experience. Larger issue volumes are resolved over time based on the monthly hours included in the plan.

How Many Monthly Maintenance Hours Can I Choose?

Managed Website Maintenance provides 1–20 dedicated web maintenance hours per month based on your website’s age, size, complexity, and condition. Monthly hours provide dedicated professional maintenance time—not a guaranteed number of resolved issues. We address the most critical needs first, then continue through the remaining issues as monthly time allows.

Are Hosting, Backups, and Uptime Monitoring Included?

Hosting, server-level backups, and uptime monitoring are not included in maintenance plans. Managed website hosting is available as a separate add-on starting at $55/month for Business Shared Hosting.

Is Technical SEO Included?

Technical SEO is available as a separate plan covering crawling, indexing, Search Console monitoring, sitemaps, schema markup, site structure, and Core Web Vitals. Advanced SEO strategy, content optimization, and AI search optimization are handled through our separate SEO + AIO services.
